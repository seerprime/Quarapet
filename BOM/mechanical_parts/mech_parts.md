## 🛠️ Usage

### M3 × 2.5 mm & M3 × 3 mm Screws
Used for securing small mechanical components, brackets, servos, and structural parts.

### Screwdriver Kit
Used for assembling and disassembling the mechanical structure and tightening Phillips-head screws.

### Glue Gun
Used for:
- Securing loose wires
- Strain relief
- Holding lightweight components
- Additional mechanical support

### Soldering Wire & Soldering Iron
Used together to create permanent electrical connections between components mounted on the perfboard and headers.

### 5 × 7 cm Perfboard
Provides a compact platform for permanently mounting and interconnecting electronic components.

### 3-Pin Header
Used to provide detachable connections for sensors, servos, modules, or other peripherals.

## ⚠️ Safety Notes

- Allow the soldering iron to heat up on a stable stand.
- Avoid touching the soldering iron tip or recently soldered joints.
- Use adequate ventilation while soldering.
- Handle the hot glue gun carefully to avoid burns.
- Check screw lengths before installation to prevent damage to internal components.
- Disconnect the power supply before modifying or soldering the circuit.
